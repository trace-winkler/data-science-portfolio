# Welcome to my Portfolio Website!

## Personal Introduction/About Me
Hello! My name is Trace, and I'm an upcoming Data Scientist with a passion for all things sports. 

Through my education at UNC Charlotte, I've learned that I have a deep desire to use Data Science and Data Visualization in order to help casual sports fans gain a deeper understanding of Sports Statistics. I truly enjoy collecting and analyzing Data that relates to sports. It's incredibly interesting to me to see how predictive models can be used to determine specific player and team performances, either for an entire season or a single game. I'd love to be able to make Statistics both accessible and understandable for anybody who has the desire to learn.

I am currently a Student Manager for the UNCC Baseball Team, and I hope to be able to use the experience I gain in my career. I've truly enjoyed the experience, and I'm excited as the team ramps up for the start of the season in February! Go Niners!

## A Short Reflection
I'm very thankful for all of the sports-related opportunities I've been able to experience during my short time as a Sports Analytics Major. My first sports job was with the [Holly Springs Salamanders](https://salamandersbaseball.com/), and it was a truly unforgettable experience. I'll never forget the first night they asked me to dress up as Santa Clause so that I could entertain the crowd (seen on the left). It wasn't what I thought I'd be doing as an intern, but it was an incredible experience that I'll cherish for the rest of my life. With the Salamanders, my love for sports truly came to bloom. I learned just how much fun it was to be apart of something bigger than myself as we competed in the playoffs for the first time in over ten years. I'm so thankful for how much I learned. 
As I move on to work with the [UNC Charlotte Baseball Team](https://charlotte49ers.com/sports/baseball), I carry all of these experiences with me. I'm incredibly excited and honored to be apart of a historical D1 baseball program, and I couldn't be happier that it's with my college. I've already learned so much with the Forty-Niners, like how to operate a trackman, and how to set up practice drills. I'm constantly inspired by the coaching staff and players, who inspire me to perform better and do my all to support the team. 
I've been incredibly lucky to meet and make friends at UNCC with some of the kindest and most down-to-earth people I've ever met. When I was coming out of high school, I had no idea what I wanted to do. I had a love for the NFL, but I never thought that I'd be able to work in sports. I tried computer science, and quickly learned that it wasn't for me. It wasn't until my second semester of my sophomore year when I took Sports Analytics, and it was like my eyes were opened. I realized that I would do whatever it would take in order for me to work in a sports-related field. It was the first time in my life that I felt like I could enjoy having a job. 
I recognize how many opportunities I've had up to this point, and I intend to make the most of everything I do. As I move towards my senior year of college, my vision for the future becomes clearer, and I'm able to see myself working for a major sports organization, doing something I love everyday.
