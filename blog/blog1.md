# Blog 1 
What is Data Science to Me?

For me, as a student, I see data science as a way to continue to revolutionize sports. I believe that people have truly figured out how to use data in order to create opportunities for athletes to be better, and smarter. However, we shouldn't rely on just data. Sports is the culmination of research, culture, and effort. As sports progress, data will continue to work hand-in-hand with the physical and mental aspects of sports.
